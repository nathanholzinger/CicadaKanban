## Proof list

### Basis and observation contract

**Prepared by the QA Engineer, 2026-11-16.** Start commit: `1a7f13bd8916b4dbec3579c54ac4e17b5ff2a518` (batch 4f committed); the working tree was clean at entry. The [baseline reconciliation](../../audits/test-reconciliation-story-10-baseline.md)'s Coverage Mapping supplies the residuals. The current cases and their cited wiki passages supply every expected result. In particular, the baseline's opacity/colour residuals are superseded by the current TREE-2 cases: TREE-2.8 owns painted appearance. No new appearance assertion is proposed.

**Inventory:** 33 assignments: 7 Extend, 18 New, 1 Rework, 6 Split and 1 Coder. The proposal is 32 surface case carriers and the existing approved internal TREE-9.4 scenario family, subject to its fresh-read condition below. These are planned carriers, not executable counts or a frozen selection. One carrier may loop over the variants its Then names; no extra origins, caps, failure modes or timing margins are added. Existing unrelated carriers are retained.

Every surface carrier first reads back its named parent links, positions and sibling order through checked seed/read helpers, and confirms the visible and explicitly hidden rows, expansion states and cap room its Given needs. Depth 0 is the top level in placement cases; absolute cap calculations count the top level as 1. The tests read the configured project cap rather than pinning today's value.

Observations have three distinct sources: the rendered sidebar (row presence/order, name position, expansion and copy/count line), attributes the app provides (`data-drag-ghost`, `data-drop-line`, `data-drop-invalid`, `data-drop-tier`), and independent saved API rows plus page requests. A helper never calculates the expected parent, slot, tier or saved outcome with the production placement/classification function. Expected tables come from the cases. Geometry uses `dragGesture.ts`'s box helpers; gesture aiming uses that file and `projectTreeDrag.ts`. A CSS offset or a ghost-depth attribute alone does not prove the painted line is at the ghost's top edge.

Held successful answers reach the real server before being released to the page. The test verifies the saved change before opening the gate; refused test writes are distinguished from the real server's 400 cycle/cap refusals. Gates are released in cleanup. Request observation begins after setup, isolates each When, and checks exact move count/body or zero moves as applicable; saved rows alone do not prove no request. Negative and continuity assertions keep a record of the observation interval: they do not retry until a forbidden state has disappeared. No test asserts a tuned duration or the copy's hold, pixel step, styling or glide.
